Key Research Empirical Findings

Zero-PII On-Chain Guarantee

Patient personal identification information (names, IDs, addresses) is completely excluded from blockchain state. Identity verification is conducted via client-side Keccak-256 canonical hashing salted with 32 cryptographically secure random bytes.

Strict Atomic Anti-Double Dispensing

Prescriptions strictly transition from ACTIVE (1) to USED (2) upon dispensing. Subsequent dispense attempts revert deterministically, eliminating pharmacy doctor shopping and prescription reuse.

Automated Cross-Component Oracle Gates

Component 4 verifies Component 2 AI Risk attestation (blocking scores ≥ 70) and Component 3 Pharmacy Trust attestation (blocking scores < 70) within atomic on-chain transaction preflights.

Sub-3ms Local Blockchain Settlement

Prescription issuance completes in ~2.65 ms (244,045 gas) and verification-dispense executes in ~2.03 ms (93,813 gas), providing instant response for clinical dispensing workflows.
